## ✅ Done (added 2026-08-24, Pricing & SEO agents go from "thinks" to "does")
User asked directly: do these agents just think, or can they act? Honest answer at the time was mixed — some agents (Retention, Fraud, Reviews, Chatbot, Personalization, Experimentation, Tax, Competitor Intel) already took real autonomous action; Pricing and SEO only wrote advisory text a human had to act on manually. Closed that gap for the two agents where it was safely closable without new integrations (Content/Ads execution still blocked on the deferred Meta OAuth flow).
- [x] **SEO Agent now really writes** — picks one real product or page each cycle and writes its real `seoTitle`/`seoDescription` directly via Prisma. Auto-apply is **on by default** (metadata-only, fully reversible, no financial risk). Verified live: real product's SEO title changed in the DB, and the storefront page's actual browser tab title changed to match — a real, user-facing effect, not just a dashboard note.
- [x] **Pricing Agent now really writes** — proposes a real new price grounded in real cost price + real competitor pricing from that cycle's scan, and can write `Product.price` directly. Auto-apply is **off by default** — real money is involved, so the owner must consciously opt in (`AgentSettings.autoApplyPricing`). Two hard, code-enforced guards apply even when on: (1) never below `minMarginPercent` (default 15%) over the real cost price, refused outright, not just discouraged; (2) never more than a 20% swing from the current real price in one cycle. Every rejection is explained honestly in the task text — never silently dropped. Verified live: with no real competitor pricing available, the agent correctly said so and made no change rather than inventing a reason to act.
- [x] New settings: `autoApplyPricing`, `autoApplySeo`, `minMarginPercent` — real, per-business, editable on each agent's detail page.
- [ ] Not yet extended to other agents: Inventory & Fulfillment still only writes advisory text (there's genuinely little for it to *do* until ad-pause or storefront-hide actions exist); Content/Ads execution still blocked on Meta OAuth (deferred).
