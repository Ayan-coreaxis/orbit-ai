# E-Commerce AI Business OS — Short Summary

Saved: 2026-08-13 · Full detail file: `ecommerce-ai-os-plan-v1.md` (same folder)

An all-in-one AI platform for online sellers, any size, any market — a store builder plus a coordinated team of ~23 AI agents (competitor analysis, content, SEO, ads, pricing, chatbot, retention, fulfillment, finance, compliance, etc.) that all take direction from one central **Strategy Orchestrator** instead of acting independently.

- **Own store builder**, not built on Shopify — global from day one.
- Plan is to **launch once the full system is complete**, not a staged rollout.
- **Pricing:** three flat-fee tiers — Starter $25, Growth $44, Scale $100 — with usage caps and top-up credits ($5 = 100 credits). WhatsApp/Meta API keys are the seller's own.
- **Market:** real research shows no single company does this today. Market is fragmented across point solutions (Klaviyo $1.5B/yr, Gorgias $69M ARR, Shopify's own AI) each huge alone. The gap — one Orchestrator coordinating all agents instead of them operating blind to each other — is the real differentiator.
- **Revenue projection:** small loss at launch (-$466/mo), profitable by ~Month 3, ~$26K/month profit (~$312K/yr run rate) by Month 24 — under conservative growth assumptions.
- **Open risk, unresolved:** ~90% of AI startups fail in year one, usually from burning runway before reaching revenue — exactly the risk the current "build everything before launch" decision creates. Next decision to make: keep the full build, or ship a smaller first slice (e.g. Competitor Analysis + Strategy Report alone) to validate demand first.
